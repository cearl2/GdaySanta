G'DAY SANTA - STRIPE TIERED PRICING CHECKLIST
==============================================

Purpose
-------
Maintain and verify Stripe checkout links that charge according to the
selected package and number of children.

Current state
-------------
- Stripe links for 1 to 5 children are in:
  cdn-cms-s-8-4.f-static.net/manager/ai_app_builder/v3/assets/js/stripe.js
- index.html loads stripe.js before forms.js.
- After Web3Forms accepts the details, forms.js redirects a single-child
  package to link 1, or a family pack to the link matching the number of
  child rows.
- The family pack allows 2 to 5 children and defaults to 3, configured by
  minFamilyChildren, maxFamilyChildren, and defaultFamilyChildren in app0ea7.js.
- The displayed family price uses calculateTotal() in app0ea7.js; forms.js
  chooses a Stripe link by child count rather than reading that function.
- The family package input still has data-price="35" in index.html. Check
  whether this fixed value is used elsewhere; update or remove it if it
  conflicts with tiered pricing.
- Form details are sent to Web3Forms before redirecting to Stripe. Web3Forms
  success does not confirm that Stripe payment was completed.

Remaining checklist
-------------------
1. Confirm each Stripe link charges the expected amount and currency:
   1 child:  AU$20
   2 children: AU$36
   3 children: AU$48
   4 children: AU$59
   5 children: AU$70
   6 children: AU$80
   7 children: AU$85
   8 children: AU$90
   9 children: AU$95
   10 children: AU$100
   Over 10: AU$100 plus AU$10 per additional child

   The family pack currently allows 2 to 5 children; a 1-child order uses the
   separate single-child package. If either family limit changes, update and
   test minFamilyChildren, maxFamilyChildren, the links map, and displayed
   pricing together.

2. Test the mapping and payment flow:
   - Single-child package redirects to link 1.
   - Family pack with 2, 3, 4, or 5 children redirects to the matching link.
   - The displayed price matches Stripe's checkout total and receipt.
   - Failed Web3Forms submissions do not redirect.
   - Canceled or failed Stripe payments are not treated as paid orders.
   - Verify form submission and payment are not duplicated.

3. Decide how to confirm paid orders:
   - Do not treat Web3Forms success as payment confirmation; it occurs before
     Stripe checkout.
   - If the site needs to record paid status or send fulfillment only after
     payment, add a verified Stripe webhook or another trusted payment
     confirmation flow.

4. Review the fixed family data-price="35" attribute in index.html and remove
   or update it if any script or service still uses it.

5. Before changing the Stripe links, replace the corresponding entries in
   stripe.js and retest every package/child-count mapping.
