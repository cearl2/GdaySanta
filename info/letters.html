Questions

Before you put the letter under the tree.

What exactly is personalised?

Your child's first name appears in the letter and on the envelope, and we weave in the age and the small detail you share — a new sibling, a lost tooth, a footy medal. We also work in the special note you send us and write a reply to what your child asked Santa for, so no two letters are ever the same.

When will the letter arrive?

Letters are posted within five business days. Standard post within Australia usually takes 2 to 6 business days after that. Order by 12 December to have it in hand before Christmas morning.

Can I order for more than one child?

Yes. Each letter is written for one child, and the family package gives every child their own letter in its own sealed envelope.

What if the letter arrives damaged or goes missing?

If your letter arrives damaged, or has not arrived within 10 business days of posting, we reprint and repost it at no charge. Just quote the order number from your confirmation.
